About School


St. John Elementary School is a public school located in Barangay Dicayas, Dipolog City, Zamboanga del Norte. It serves the children and families in the local community by providing a safe and welcoming place for learning. The school provides free and quality education to children in the community. It offers lessons and activities that help students improve their knowledge, skills, and understanding of different subjects. St. John Elementary School also aims to develop good values and positive attitudes among its students. Through the support of teachers, parents, and the community, the school helps children become responsible, respectful, and productive individuals.
